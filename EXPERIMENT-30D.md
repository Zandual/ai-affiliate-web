# 30-Day AI Stack Finder Experiment

**Window:** October 7 – November 6, 2026  
**Branch:** `experiment/ai-stack-finder-30d`  
**Goal:** Reach first genuine affiliate revenue by solving an existing small-business problem with a focused AI-tool recommendation funnel.

## Audience
U.S. small-business owners and solopreneurs who are overwhelmed by AI-tool choices, overlapping features, and recurring subscriptions.

## Core promise
**Choose a smaller AI stack that actually fits your work — not 20 subscriptions you barely use.**

## Customer questions the site should answer
1. What job am I trying to accomplish?
2. Which 1–3 AI tools are appropriate?
3. Why choose one over another?
4. What will it cost?
5. What can I skip?

## Funnel v1
Traffic source → focused landing page → problem/category selection → honest 1–3 tool recommendation → pros/cons + “best for / skip if” → outbound affiliate click → provider-reported conversion.

## Measurement
Track independently:
- visits / sessions
- product-card impressions
- outbound affiliate clicks
- click-through rate
- provider-reported signups / conversions
- revenue

A click is **not** a conversion.

## Initial monetization candidates
Only use currently verified programs and never fabricate affiliate URLs.

- Writesonic
- ElevenLabs
- Descript, if it fits the audience

Affiliate URLs remain configuration placeholders until the owner has the approved referral links.

## Constraints
- zero / near-zero startup cost
- no paid ads during initial validation
- no fake testimonials or fabricated earnings
- no speculative trading
- no affiliate-program enrollment or legal acceptance without owner action
- keep production changes reversible and reviewable

## First content hypothesis
**The 3-tool AI stack for a small business: what to pay for, what to skip, and where tools overlap.**

Use actual customer language from reviews, forums, comments, and search intent before writing sales copy.

## 30-day success ladder
1. Funnel deployed and measurable.
2. First qualified outbound clicks.
3. First provider-reported signup/conversion.
4. First genuine affiliate revenue.
5. One repeatable acquisition/content channel identified.

## End-of-experiment review
On November 6, record:
- traffic by source
- impressions
- outbound clicks
- CTR
- provider conversions
- revenue
- best/worst content
- customer-language findings
- what to keep, change, or kill
